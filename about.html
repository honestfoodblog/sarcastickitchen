About Me

The person behind the recipes

Hi, I’m Alex. Welcome to Sarcastic Kitchen.

I started this site because I was tired of recipe blogs that felt more like novels than cooking instructions. You know the ones — three paragraphs about a childhood memory before you even see the ingredients list. I wanted something simpler: honest food, clear steps, and a little sarcasm on the side.

Most of what you’ll find here is weeknight-friendly. I’m a big fan of the Instant Pot, one-pan dinners, and anything that doesn’t require dirtying every dish in the kitchen. You’ll also see the occasional weekend project (looking at you, overnight focaccia) when I’m feeling ambitious.

I’m not a professional chef. I’m just someone who likes feeding people good food without the pretense. If a recipe works, I share it. If it doesn’t, I quietly delete it and pretend it never happened.

Thanks for stopping by. Hope something here makes it onto your table.

— Alex
